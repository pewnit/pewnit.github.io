QA Wolf

Working at QA Wolf was probably the best thing that could've happened to me, and I'm grateful for the 2+ years I spent there.

What did I learn?

  • Knowing how to solve problems isn't the best skill to have. Knowing how to find out how to solve problems is.
  • Pleasing your customers is the most key factor to a successful business. This doesn't mean that every demand should be met, but just like every relationship, communication is key.
  • You'll always have people to help you when you're stuck, you just need to ask for help. Learning when to ask for help is key.
